Choosing keys that work
  • Single keys are fine for marking, since they only apply while a popup is open.
  • Add Ctrl, Shift, or Alt for anything that fires page-wide.
  • Two Helios shortcuts cannot share a combination.
  • Chrome keeps Ctrl+W, Ctrl+T, Ctrl+N, and F1–F12 for itself.
