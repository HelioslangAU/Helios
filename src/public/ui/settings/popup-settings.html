Staying open

Whether the card waits for you after you let go of the activation key.

The card stays up after you release the activation key, so you can click into it. Off, it closes the moment you let go.

sec

Closes the card on its own after this many seconds. Zero leaves it open until you move away. Anything above 30 is refused.
