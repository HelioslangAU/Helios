While an entry is open

These keys act on the word in the open card. Click a key box and press the combination you want; it is saved as soon as you press it.

Puts the word back in the flagged list.

Stops flagging the word without counting it as learned — for names and words you do not intend to study.

Adds the word to your known list and unflags it everywhere.

Keeps the word flagged but records that you are working on it.

Creates a card in the deck set up under Anki integration.

Subtitles

These work on any page with a video, whether or not a card is open.

Opens the file picker for an .srt or .vtt file.

Shows or hides the transcript beside the player.

Pulls the captions YouTube already has for the video you are watching.

The keys that step between subtitles — A, S, D and W — are set under Video.

What a key can be

  • A single key, or a key with Ctrl, Shift or Alt held.
  • Not a key already bound above; the box turns red and names the clash.
  • Not a key Chrome has taken: Ctrl+W, Ctrl+T, Ctrl+N, and F1–F12.
  • Click away from a box to leave its key as it was.
