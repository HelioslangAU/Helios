Stepping through subtitles

Keys that move the video by one subtitle at a time. Click a key box and press the combination you want.

With this off, the four keys below do nothing and the player keeps its own shortcuts.

Jumps back to the start of the previous line.

Jumps forward to the start of the next line.

Returns to the beginning of the line now playing.

Hides the Helios subtitle overlay without unloading it.

Hold a modifier if a single letter fights the player’s own keys. Shift+A and Alt+D are safe on YouTube.

Playback

When Helios is allowed to stop and start the video for you.

Pauses as soon as you hover a subtitle word, and resumes when you move away.

Stops at every subtitle boundary, which is what you want for shadowing.

Starts playing again when you step to another line while paused. Otherwise stepping leaves the video paused on the new line.

Placing the subtitles

Size and position are set on the video itself, and both are remembered between videos.

  • Shift+= makes the subtitles larger, Shift+− smaller.
  • Drag the subtitle text to put it anywhere over the picture.
  • Double-click it to drop it back to the centre.
