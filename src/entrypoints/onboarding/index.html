Helios

/ˈhiː.li.əs/ n.

  1. A bilingual dictionary that reads the page with you. Hold Shift over any word and its entry opens where you are already looking.
  2. Words you have marked as known stay plain. Everything else is flagged, so you can see what a page will cost you before you start reading it.
  3. Six steps set your two languages, your level, and the one key you need.
